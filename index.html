Job Market Analysis Dashboard — 2025

Excel / Business Intelligence

An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing a 2025 Indian job-market dataset to understand demand, salary, location and skill patterns.

Business Question

What can job-market data tell us about employment opportunities, salary patterns, locations, work modes, industries and skills in 2025?

Approach

Cleaned and prepared 420 job listings, then used pivot tables, pivot charts, KPI cards and lookup/conditional functions (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS) to build an interactive dashboard.

Key Insights

  • Sales Executive was among the most frequently listed roles in the dataset analyzed
  • Bengaluru was the leading location for job opportunities
  • Average salary generally increased with experience level
  • Remote roles made up a smaller share of listings compared to hybrid and on-site

Web Data Analysis

Python

Analysis of website traffic and user engagement data to understand how visitors interact with a site.

Problem

Which traffic channels bring the most users, and do they actually engage once they arrive?

Approach

Cleaned and explored the dataset, then visualized traffic and engagement patterns using line graphs, bar charts, box plots and heatmaps in Matplotlib and Seaborn.

Key Insights

  • Direct and Organic Social channels brought the highest number of users
  • Organic Social users showed stronger engagement than other channels
  • High traffic did not always translate into high engagement
